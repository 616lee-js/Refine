## US Crisis Resources (v1 starter list)

**988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline**
Call or text 988 (US). Available 24/7. For people in emotional distress or suicidal crisis.
Chat: 988lifeline.org

**Crisis Text Line**
Text HOME to 741741 (US). Free, 24/7 crisis counseling via text.

**SAMHSA National Helpline**
1-800-662-4357. Free, confidential, 24/7. Substance use and mental health referrals.
samhsa.gov/find-help/national-helpline

**Warmlines (peer support — not crisis)**
Warmlines are staffed by people with lived mental health experience. Lower threshold than crisis lines — for when you need to talk but aren't in crisis.
Directory: warmline.org

**Sliding-scale therapy directories**
- Open Path Collective: openpathcollective.org — sessions $30–$80
- Inclusive Therapists: inclusivetherapists.com — BIPOC, LGBTQ+ affirming focus
- Psychology Today: psychologytoday.com/us/therapists — filter by sliding scale

## Note to Claude

These resources are for Tier 2 and Tier 3 situations. At Tier 2: offer them alongside continued presence — do not make continued conversation contingent on accepting them. At Tier 3: surface them clearly and directly. In both cases, the app remains a place the user can return to.
